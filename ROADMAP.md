# Gemstone Guilds - Development Roadmap

**Last Updated:** May 2, 2026  
**Project:** Gemstone Guilds Multiplayer Card Game Platform  
**Planning Horizon:** 18 Months

---

## Vision Statement

Create the premier web-based multiplayer card gaming platform that delivers engaging, accessible gaming experiences with a thriving social community, fair progression systems, and sustainable monetization—expanding the portfolio of games while maintaining quality and player satisfaction.

## Dependencies & Risks

### Key Dependencies
- Stable player base growth (organic + marketing)
- Team scalability (developers, community managers, support)
- Third-party service reliability (analytics, hosting, payment processors)
- Market demand for card games

### Identified Risks & Mitigation
| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|-----------|
| Slow user adoption | Project viability at risk | Medium | Early community building, content marketing, streamer partnerships |
| Server outages during peaks | Revenue loss, reputation | Medium | Load testing, geographic redundancy, monitoring alerts |
| Competitive games launch | Market share erosion | High | Unique features (social, cosmetics), strong community, regular updates |
| Monetization resistance | Insufficient revenue | Medium | Fair pricing, cosmetics-only model, transparent economy |
| Key team member departure | Development delays | Low-Medium | Documentation, knowledge sharing, cross-training |
| Payment processor issues | Revenue interruption | Low | Multiple payment integrations, backup processors |
| Regulatory/legal issues | Platform shutdown | Low | Legal review, GDPR/privacy compliance, age verification system |
