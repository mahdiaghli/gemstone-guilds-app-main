# Gemstone Guilds - Stakeholder Interview Guide

**Last Updated:** May 2, 2026  
**Project:** Gemstone Guilds Multiplayer Card Game Platform

---

## Overview

This document provides structured interview templates and guidelines for conducting interviews with key stakeholders in the Gemstone Guilds project. These interviews are essential for gathering requirements, validating assumptions, and understanding user needs at various stages of development.

---

## Pre-Interview Best Practices

### Before the Interview
- [ ] Define clear interview objectives
- [ ] Identify the right stakeholders
- [ ] Schedule 30-60 minutes (respect their time)
- [ ] Prepare questions in advance
- [ ] Send context/background information
- [ ] Record (with permission) for accuracy
- [ ] Create comfortable environment

### During the Interview
- [ ] Start with rapport building (2 min)
- [ ] Explain the purpose and structure (2 min)
- [ ] Ask open-ended questions first
- [ ] Listen more than you talk (80/20 rule)
- [ ] Take notes on key points
- [ ] Ask follow-up "why" questions
- [ ] Probe for pain points and needs

### After the Interview
- [ ] Thank them and confirm next steps
- [ ] Summarize key findings within 24 hours
- [ ] Share findings with core team
- [ ] Follow up on action items
- [ ] Document insights for future reference
- [ ] Look for patterns across multiple interviews

## 1. User/Player Interview Guide

**Duration:** 45-60 minutes  
**Target:** New players, active players, lapsed players  
**Goal:** Understand player motivations, pain points, and desires

### 1.1 Icebreaker & Context (5 min)
```
1. Tell me about your gaming background. What games do you typically enjoy?
2. How long have you been playing Gemstone Guilds (or similar games)?
3. On a scale of 1-10, how would you rate your interest in card games?
```

### 1.2 Discovery & Onboarding (8 min)
```
4. How did you first hear about Gemstone Guilds?
5. What appealed to you most about trying this game?
6. Tell me about your first experience with the game.
   - Was the tutorial helpful?
   - Were there any confusing mechanics?
   - How long did it take before you felt comfortable playing?
7. What would have made onboarding smoother?
```

### 1.3 Gameplay Experience (12 min)
```
8. Which game mode do you prefer playing? (Solo, Multiplayer, Challenges)
   - Why do you prefer this mode?
9. How often do you play, and how long are typical sessions?
10. Describe your favorite Gemstone Guilds moment.
11. What makes you keep coming back to the game?
12. What frustrates you most about the gameplay?
13. Are there any game mechanics you find confusing or broken?
14. How do you feel about the game's difficulty/balance?
    - Against AI opponents?
    - Against real players?
15. Would you prefer faster or slower games? Why?
```

### 1.4 Monetization & Shop (7 min)
```
16. Have you made any purchases in the game?
    - If yes: What did you buy? Was it worth the cost?
    - If no: What would convince you to spend money?
17. What cosmetics would you like to see?
18. Do you feel the shop prices are fair?
19. Have you felt pressure or resentment toward paying players?
```

### 1.5 Social Features (7 min)
```
20. Do you play with friends or in a guild?
    - How does this enhance (or detract from) your experience?
21. Have you made new friends through the game?
22. What social features would improve your experience?
    - Better chat? Spectating? Leaderboards?
23. Do you use voice chat or audio features?
```

### 1.6 Technical & Accessibility (5 min)
```
24. Have you experienced any technical issues?
    - Crashes? Lag? Connection drops?
25. How's your experience on mobile vs. desktop?
26. Have you had difficulty with the language or UI?
27. Any accessibility features you'd like to see?
```

### 1.7 Future & Retention (7 min)
```
28. What would make you stop playing Gemstone Guilds?
29. What features would make you play more often?
30. Would you recommend this game to others? Why/why not?
31. If you could add one feature, what would it be?
32. Any other feedback or suggestions?
```

### 1.8 Closing (2 min)
```
Thank you for your time! We'll share key findings in a few weeks. 
Do you have any questions for us?
```

## 2. Team Member Interviews

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes  
**Target:** Developers, designers, community managers  
**Goal:** Understand team dynamics, blockers, and alignment

### 2.1 Role & Responsibilities (5 min)
```
1. Tell me about your role in Gemstone Guilds.
2. What are your primary responsibilities?
3. What aspects of your role do you find most rewarding?
4. What's your biggest challenge currently?
```

### 2.2 Technical/Creative Challenges (10 min)
```
5. What technical debt or design issues are you tracking?
6. Where do you see the biggest architectural challenges ahead?
7. Are you satisfied with the current development process?
8. What tools or resources would help you be more effective?
9. Are there any features you'd prioritize differently?
```

### 2.3 Team Dynamics (8 min)
```
10. How well does the team collaborate?
11. Are there communication breakdowns between teams (backend, frontend, design)?
12. What's working well in our team structure?
13. What could we improve about how we work together?
14. Do you feel heard when you raise concerns or ideas?
```

### 2.4 Project Direction (7 min)
```
15. Do you understand our long-term vision for Gemstone Guilds?
16. Are you aligned with the current roadmap priorities?
17. What do you think we should focus on next?
18. Where do you see the biggest risks to our success?
19. Are there team members or roles you think we're missing?
```

### 2.5 Professional Growth (5 min)
```
20. What are your career goals?
21. Are there skills you'd like to develop?
22. Do you see opportunities for growth and advancement?
23. How satisfied are you overall with your role?
```

### 2.6 Closing (2 min)
```
Is there anything else you'd like to discuss? Any concerns I haven't asked about?
```

## 3. Stakeholder/Executive Interview Guide

**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Target:** Product owners, executives, investors  
**Goal:** Validate vision, business metrics, and strategic alignment

### 3.1 Vision & Goals (7 min)
```
1. What is your vision for Gemstone Guilds?
2. What are the top 3 success metrics for the next 12 months?
3. How does Gemstone Guilds fit into the broader gaming market?
4. What's our competitive advantage?
```

### 3.2 Business Model (5 min)
```
5. How do we plan to monetize the game?
6. What's our target ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)?
7. What's the timeline to profitability?
8. Have you validated the market demand for this game?
```

### 3.3 User Acquisition & Growth (5 min)
```
9. What's our user acquisition strategy?
10. What's our target DAU/MAU at key milestones (3mo, 6mo, 12mo)?
11. Do we have a marketing budget allocated?
12. Are we leveraging any partnerships or influencers?
```

### 3.4 Risks & Opportunities (5 min)
```
13. What are your biggest concerns about this project?
14. What opportunities do you see that we haven't exploited yet?
15. Are there regulatory or legal issues we should be aware of?
16. How will we respond if a major competitor launches?
```

### 3.5 Resource & Timeline (3 min)
```
17. Are we adequately resourced to hit the roadmap milestones?
18. What's your expected timeline to product market fit?
19. Should we be preparing for Series A/scaling investment?
```

## 4. AI Testing Interview Guide

**Duration:** 15-30 minutes (multiple rounds)  
**Target:** Players testing AI opponents  
**Goal:** Validate AI difficulty and behavior

### 4.1 Pre-Game (2 min)
```
1. Which difficulty are you testing? [Easy / Medium / Hard]
2. How many games have you played so far?
```

### 4.2 During & Post-Game (10-20 min)
```
3. How would you rate the AI's difficulty?
   - Too easy / Too hard / Just right
4. Did the AI make intelligent decisions?
5. Did the AI cheat or behave unfairly?
6. Was the game pace appropriate?
7. Did the AI have obvious exploitable patterns?
8. How did this compare to playing against other players?
9. What should we adjust about this difficulty level?
```

### 4.3 Behavioral Observations
```
10. Check boxes for observed behaviors:
    [ ] AI prioritizes victory points aggressively
    [ ] AI saves resources intelligently
    [ ] AI blocks player purchases
    [ ] AI reserves cards strategically
    [ ] AI takes unnecessary tokens
    [ ] AI makes obviously bad moves
```

**Target:** Internal/external testers

## 11. Research Ethics & Best Practices

### Informed Consent
- [ ] Clearly explain the purpose of the interview
- [ ] Mention if/how data will be recorded
- [ ] Explain confidentiality and how findings will be used
- [ ] Obtain permission before recording

### Data Privacy
- [ ] Store recordings securely
- [ ] De-identify responses in reports
- [ ] Don't share raw interview transcripts without consent
- [ ] Delete recordings after 90 days (unless exceptionally valuable)

### Inclusivity
- [ ] Recruit diverse participants (age, gender, region, ability)
- [ ] Offer incentives to offset their time
- [ ] Conduct interviews in accessible formats
- [ ] Be aware of language/cultural differences

### Bias Mitigation
- [ ] Use open-ended questions to avoid leading
- [ ] Don't telegraph "correct" answers
- [ ] Interview people with diverse opinions
- [ ] Be aware of your own biases
- [ ] Look for disconfirming evidence
